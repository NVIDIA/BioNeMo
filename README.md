# NVIDIA Clara

Welcome to NVIDIA Clara, a collection of open-source models, tools, and recipes designed to accelerate scientific discovery and medical AI development to advance our foundational understanding of human health, physiology, biology, and chemistry. 

We offer open models and tools that span the drug discovery and development and medical AI development lifecycles.

This repository is the central hub for the NVIDIA Clara resources. Our open-source work is organized into two primary areas:

## 1. Digital Biology [(NVIDIA-Digital-Bio)](https://github.com/NVIDIA-Digital-Bio)

* Explore our open models and tools for Digital Biology at: [https://github.com/NVIDIA-Digital-Bio](https://github.com/NVIDIA-Digital-Bio)
* **What you’ll find:** Models and tools for biology and molecular science.

## 2. Medical AI [(NVIDIA-Medtech)](https://github.com/nvidia-medtech)

* Explore our open models and tools for Medtech: [https://github.com/nvidia-medtech](https://github.com/nvidia-medtech)  
* **What you’ll find:** Models and workflows for physical modalities spanning medical imaging, digital surgery, and healthcare robotics.
